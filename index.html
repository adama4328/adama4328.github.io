✨ What is Goal-to-Game?

Goal-to-Game is a powerful yet simple application that lets you build your own video games using high-quality 3D assets created by artificial intelligence. Whether you want to create a simple puzzle game, an adventure world, or just experiment with 3D graphics, this tool makes it possible for anyone - even complete beginners.

The application works with two amazing technologies: Thrixel for generating 3D objects and Claude Code for adding intelligent game logic. But don't worry - you don't need to understand how they work internally. Everything is handled automatically for you.

Step 1: Define Your Vision

In the main window, type a short description of your game. For example: "A space explorer collecting crystals on a distant planet."

Step 2: Let AI Generate Assets

Click "Generate" and watch as Goal-to-Game creates 3D models for your space explorer, the crystal diamonds, and the planet terrain. Everything looks beautiful right out of the box.

Step 3: Add Game Logic

Describe how you want your game to work. Say: "Click on crystals to collect them. Player moves left and right with arrow keys." The AI will generate all the necessary game code.

Step 4: Preview & Play

Hit "Play" to test your game instantly. You can make changes, regenerate assets, and tweak everything in real-time until it feels perfect.
